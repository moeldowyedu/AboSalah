Who I am

An engineer who kept
the CEO chair warm.

I started in code and never left it. The title changed—senior developer, project manager, CEO—but the job stayed the same: find where a business loses time, and build the system that gives it back.

Today that means agentic AI: workflows where software decides, acts, and reports instead of waiting for someone to click. Underneath it is the same discipline I've taught at ITI for over a decade—clean architecture, honest data models, APIs that don't surprise you at 2 a.m.

I work best where the stakes are operational: the process that breaks at scale, the integration nobody wants to own, the platform that has to survive its own success.

Good software is the kind nobody has to talk about after launch.
